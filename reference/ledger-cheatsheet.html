Back-dated Payment

Insert it with its real paid_on. The next Replay folds it in, and every later step changes, including the splits of untouched later Payments. If it makes any Allocation larger than what was owed on that Allocation's date, the write is refused.

An Accrual Date, in order · lesson 2

① Payments dated before today
② interest = rate(Terms of the month ending) × Principal at that month's start
③ new month starts: month_start_principal = Principal  (before today's Payments)
④ today's Payments
⑤ capitalise whatever is still unpaid (if the ending month's Terms compound)
⑥ new Terms (Term Change) apply from this month on

Rule A: Principal paid mid-month lowers interest only from the next month (ADR 0006).
Rule B: paying on an Accrual Date stops that day's interest compounding, but the month that has just started is still charged on the old Principal.
Term Change on date D: interest charged on D still uses the old Terms.
Accrual Dates are counted from the start date and clamped to month-end: 31 Jan → 28 Feb → 31 Mar.
