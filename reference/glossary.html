Glossary

Quoted from the project's GLOSSARY.md, which wins if the two ever differ. Words to avoid are in grey.

Ledger
The step-by-step history of a Mortgage (interest added, capitalisations, payments), derived from its terms and payments. avoid: Statement, MortgageLedger
Replay
Working out a Mortgage's Ledger or Outstanding from its stored facts (its Terms, Term Changes, Closure and the Allocations of its non-Voided Payments) rather than storing them (ADR 0001). avoid: Recalculation, recompute, refresh
Allocation
The portion of a Payment applied to one Mortgage. Within a Mortgage it pays unpaid interest, then the Interest to Date (paid ahead of that month's interest), then Principal. avoid: Split, contribution
Void
Cancelling a recorded Payment with a reason, keeping it on record but excluding it from every Ledger. avoid: Delete, reverse
Term Change
A dated change to some of a Mortgage's Terms, starting on an Accrual Date and applying to the months from then on. It never rewrites earlier interest.
Principal
The amount on a Mortgage on which interest is charged. It grows when unpaid interest is capitalised and shrinks when payments exceed interest.
Accrual Date
The monthly day on which a month's interest is added, charged on the Principal as it stood when that month began (at the previous Accrual Date, before that day's Payments). It is taken from the start date and clamped to month-end when the month is shorter. avoid: Due date, interest date
Interest to Date
The interest for the month under way, as of a date, under its Part-Month Rule. It is counted from the month's first day through that date, so today is always charged. It is fixed at a Closure. avoid: Accrued interest, pending interest
Outstanding
What a Mortgage owes on a date: Principal plus unpaid interest plus its Interest to Date, so exactly what would redeem it that day. Nothing once closed. avoid: Redemption Amount, balance, payoff
