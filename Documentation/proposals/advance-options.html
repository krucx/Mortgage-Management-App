Advance: prototypes

Working prototype, not the app. The Mortgage is "Gold chain and 2 bangles", Principal 5,00,000 at 2% a month (10,000 a month), accruing on the 5th. 10,000 of interest is unpaid (the month ending 5 Nov). Received 19 Nov 2026. The figures use a simplified engine: each part pays the month ending on its date, then Principal. The real Preview would come from Replay.

Try a scenario:
A. The allocation table you already have gets two more columns, Advance and Months. Only one row may have an Advance. Amount received = pay-now + Advance. The schedule appears under the table. It's the smallest change, but the table gets wide on a phone.

A+. Any Mortgage row can have its own Advance and Months. Each one splits equally over that Mortgage's Accrual Dates: the 5th for the chain, the 12th for the ear tops. Amount received = all Pay now + all Advances. Below the table there's one schedule per Mortgage, each with its own warning, and Save is refused if any Advance would hold more than its Mortgage owes today. The scenario buttons above don't apply here. Type in the table instead.

B. A switch at the top: Ordinary keeps today's form untouched. With Advance narrows the form to one Mortgage and shows three fields: Pay now, Advance and Months. The page explains itself best, and the ordinary form never changes. It costs a second form layout.
