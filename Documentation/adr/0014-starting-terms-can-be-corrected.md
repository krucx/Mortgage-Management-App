# Starting Terms can be corrected

A Mortgage's original rate and interest rules can be recorded incorrectly, so Edit Mortgage can correct its starting Terms and replay every existing Payment under them. Keep later Term Changes as dated facts, including their existing carry-forward behavior; replay refuses the correction if any existing Payment exceeds what was owed on its date, and the Preview shows the Outstanding before and after. A Mortgage exposes both its starting Terms and the Terms currently in force so corrections cannot accidentally use a later Term Change as the starting point.
