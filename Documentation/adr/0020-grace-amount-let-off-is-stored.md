# The amount a Grace Period let off is stored, not replayed

A Payment dated earlier under a Grace Period keeps the date it was received and, for each Mortgage, the amount the earlier date let off, as worked out when the Payment was saved. This breaks from ADR 0001's rule that Ledger figures are replayed rather than stored, but the amount is a record of what the Lender decided that day, shown to the Customer as a favour; it never feeds any Ledger figure, which still replays from the Payment's (earlier) date alone. Replaying it instead would need a second Replay at the received date for every Grace Payment, and the figure would shift whenever an earlier Payment was Voided or Terms corrected, so the Lender could no longer point to what was actually let off. Changing the Grace Period setting later does not rewrite past Payments.
