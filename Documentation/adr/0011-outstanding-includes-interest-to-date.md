# Outstanding includes the Interest to Date, today included

Outstanding used to be the interest already charged on Accrual Dates plus Principal, and a separate Redemption Amount added the Part-Month Rule charge on top; Customers were told one figure and asked to pay another. Outstanding is now exactly what would redeem the Mortgage that day: it includes the Interest to Date for the month under way, counted from that month's first day (the start or an Accrual Date) through the date itself, so a Full-month Mortgage of 3000 at 3% started today owes 3090 at once, and on each Accrual Date the new month is charged as well as the one just ended. The Interest to Date is a live step at the end of an Active Mortgage's Ledger, recomputed on every Replay (ADR 0001), charged on the start-of-month Principal (ADR 0006), never capitalised, and settled into the month's interest on the next Accrual Date; Months Unpaid ignores it. A Payment pays unpaid interest, then the Interest to Date, then Principal, so what it pays of the Interest to Date is paid ahead and counts against the month's interest when charged. A month starts at the start of its Accrual Date, before that day's Payments (ADR 0006), so paying the Principal on an Accrual Date cannot let off the month starting then. The term Redemption Amount is retired, and so is Ready to Redeem, since paying the whole Outstanding Redeems.

We considered keeping the old day count for Closures dated before this change, behind a stored cutover date, so Replay would give every past Closure its old figures. We chose instead to let Replay apply the new count everywhere, with one rule that keeps old Redemptions settled: at a Redemption, the Interest to Date is capped at what the Final Payment left for it, so a Redeemed Mortgage never owes anything. Closed Mortgages already refuse Voids and edits until Reopened, so the cap cannot hide a new shortfall. Waived Amounts and Forfeiture profit or loss from before the change are rewritten by up to one day (pro-rata) or one month (Full-month on an Accrual Date). We accepted that, rather than keep two day counts in the engine forever.
