Chapter 004

The Ledger engine

The problem this chapter solves

Every money figure the app shows comes out of one place: lending/ledger.py. It covers every month's interest, how each Payment divides between interest and Principal, when unpaid interest starts earning interest, and what the Mortgage will owe a year from now. In chapter 003 it was a black box that answered "₹1,500 to interest, ₹3,500 to Principal". If that answer is ever wrong, a Customer is asked for the wrong money. So whoever changes this file has to be able to work out the right answer by hand.

The rules are small, but the order they run in matters. Should a Payment made on the 10th count before the 10th's interest, or after it? Is a month charged on the Principal at its start or at its end? Is unpaid interest capitalised before that day's Payment, or after it? Each question has one answer, recorded in an ADR, and a two-line change to accrue_through can quietly flip it. This chapter teaches those answers on an open (Active) Mortgage. Closing one is chapter 005.

What you'll be able to do

  • Say what is stored and what is derived, and why a back-dated Payment rewrites Ledger steps nobody edited.
  • Work out any month's interest charge, and any Interest to Date, by hand from the Terms.
  • Hand-trace a run of Payments in the engine's order, including one made on an Accrual Date and one made mid-month.
  • Say when unpaid interest is capitalised, and what a Customer can pay to avoid it.
  • Say when a Term Change starts to bite, and read a Projection.

Before you start

Chapter 001, lessons 2 and 3 explained the Terms, interest, Capitalisation and Payments in plain words. Here you meet the same rules as code. Chapter 003, lesson 4 showed where the Ledger is read from (load_payment → replays → compute_ledger). Keep the sample book served from chapter 002, lesson 4 (http://127.0.0.1:8765, held on 19 Oct 2026). You'll check figures on its Mortgage pages, and run short uv run python snippets from backend/ for dates the book can't reach.

The map

Notice that a Ledger and a Projection run the same _Run. A Projection is just a replay that keeps going past today. There's no second set of rules to keep in step.
