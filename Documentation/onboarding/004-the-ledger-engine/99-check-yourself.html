Quiz: trace it by hand

Several questions use Arjun's gold chain: ₹45,000 lent on 19 Aug 2026, on the sample book's Default Terms (2% a month, Compounded Yearly, pro-rata), with no Payments yet. It accrues on the 19th, and 19 Oct is one of its Accrual Dates.

1. What is the chain's monthly interest charge?

A basis point is 0.01%, so 200 bps is 2%, per month: 2% of ₹45,000 is ₹900 (trap #6). In the API it's 90000 paise.

2. On 19 Oct, after October's interest, what is the chain's Interest to Date?

Today always counts: interest_to_date_days is 1 on an Accrual Date, and pro-rata gives 1/30 of ₹900. So the chain owes ₹45,000 + ₹1,800 + ₹30 = ₹46,830. Only "full month" would give ₹900.

3. Instead, Arjun had paid ₹1,000 on 18 Oct. ₹900 was unpaid and the Interest to Date was 30 days' worth. What is unpaid just after the 19 Oct interest?

₹1,000 pays September's ₹900, then ₹100 of the Interest to Date ahead. On 19 Oct the month's ₹900 is charged less the ₹100 paid ahead: ₹800 unpaid. Nothing reaches the Principal. (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section 99.)

4. The chain is Compounded Yearly. If Arjun never pays, when is unpaid interest first capitalised?

_capitalises tests n % 12 == 0, with n counted from the start date. The first anniversary is 19 Aug 2027. Compounded Monthly would have started on 19 Sep 2026.

5. Meera's chain: ₹80,000, ₹2,800 unpaid on 10 Oct after the interest. She pays ₹50,000 on 10 Oct. What is the 10 Nov interest charged on?

The month from 10 Oct starts before that day's Payments (ADR 0006), on ₹80,000. Her ₹47,147 of Principal lowers interest from the month after, charged on 10 Dec. Lesson 3, nuance (b).

6. Meera's bangles go to 1.5% from 25 Oct. Which interest is the first one charged at 1.5%?

The interest added on 25 Oct is for the month that began on 25 Sep, under 2%: ₹3,000. The month starting 25 Oct is the first under 1.5%, and it's added on 25 Nov. On 19 Oct nothing has changed yet.

7. Kavita's last slip, dated 25 Aug, was typed after her 26 Aug Payment. What changes in the database for the 26 Aug Payment?

There is no split to update. The next replay sorts Allocations by date and finds the 26 Aug ₹300 now pays Principal. Trap #4: nothing owed is stored.

8. On a Compounded Yearly Mortgage's anniversary, ₹4,400 is unpaid after that day's interest. ₹4,400 is paid that same day. What is capitalised?

On each Accrual Date the order is interest, then Payments, then Capitalisation of what's still unpaid. Paying on the day itself avoids it. Lesson 4, nuance (a).

Hands-on: break ADR 0006 and see who notices

Recap

  • Only facts are stored. compute_ledger replays them from the start date, in date order, every time, so a back-dated Payment rewrites later steps nobody edited (lesson 1, ADR 0001, trap #4).
  • A month's charge is principal × bps × days ÷ 30, in paise, rounded half-up to the rupee. Accrual Dates come from the start date, clamped to month-end. The Part-Month Rule decides the days for a month under way (lesson 2, trap #6).
  • On each Accrual Date: earlier Payments, the month's interest on its starting Principal, the new month starts, today's Payments, then Capitalisation. Each Payment pays unpaid interest, then the Interest to Date ahead, then Principal (lesson 3, ADRs 0006 and 0011, trap #7).
  • Capitalisation moves what's still unpaid after the day's Payments into the Principal, and into the new month's starting Principal. Paid-ahead interest and the Interest to Date never capitalise (lesson 4).
  • A Term Change applies to months that start on or after its date. A Projection is the same replay continued past today, with coming Term Changes and optional made-up Payments after the real ones (lesson 5).
