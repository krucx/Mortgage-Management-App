Chapter 005

Replay and Closures

The problem this chapter solves

Chapter 004 taught the engine on an open Mortgage. Every pledge ends, though. Kavita takes her coin home, Farida is let off ₹180, and Imran's earrings are sold. Each ending has to answer three questions exactly. How much does it take to close the Mortgage on a given date? What does the record say about the money: what was paid, what was let off, what the jewellery fetched? And once it's closed, how do we make sure it never owes anything again, and that no interest keeps growing on it?

Without one place that answers these, the screens disagree. The Mortgage page says one figure and the Redeem form asks for another. That really happened before ADR 0011: Customers "were told one figure and asked to pay another". Today one class, MortgageReplay, answers every question about what a Mortgage owes. A handful of functions in changes.py record each Closure, and the same compute_ledger you met in chapter 004 replays closed Mortgages too.

What you'll be able to do

  • Say what a Mortgage's Outstanding is on any date, and which function every screen gets it from.
  • Quote what closing would take on a date, by hand and through the API, and say why a close is refused.
  • Follow both ways a Mortgage gets Redeemed, and say why paying exactly the Outstanding is never an ordinary Payment.
  • Say what a Waiver and a Forfeiture write off, and read every field of a Closure.
  • Spot a Dormant Mortgage, say why the app doesn't Redeem it for you, and Reopen a Closure on scratch data.

Before you start

Chapter 001, lesson 4 told the four October endings in plain words. Here you meet the code behind each one. Chapter 004 taught compute_ledger on an open Mortgage: the order inside a day, the Interest to Date, and why a month is charged on its starting Principal. This chapter assumes all of it. Keep the sample book served from chapter 002, lesson 4 (http://127.0.0.1:8765, held on 19 Oct 2026), with $BOOK set to its scratch folder. Build it fresh into a new empty folder if you saved chapter 003's Payment on your old one, because that Payment changes Meera's figures. Lessons 1–4 only read the book or ask for Previews, which save nothing. Lesson 5 makes two Reopens on that scratch copy, which do change it. Never run them against real data.

The map

Notice that nothing on the right of the database stores a figure. A Closure stores only facts: its kind, its date, its Final Payment's id, and for a Forfeiture the Recovered Amount. What it came to is replayed, like everything else (ADR 0001).
