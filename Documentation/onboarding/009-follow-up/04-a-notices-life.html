Chapter 009 · Lesson 4

A Notice's life: approve, skip, send

Sunita's Notice #58 is waiting for the Lender. This lesson approves it, sends it, and then records a Payment from Sunita, to show exactly what the database keeps at each step, and why the message she was sent never changes afterwards.

By the end you can follow a Notice from awaiting approval to sent or skipped, and say what is stored at each step.

The problem

A Notice's figures depend on the Ledger. Until it is sent, they should be as fresh as possible. If Sunita pays ₹5,000 this afternoon, a Notice sent tomorrow should say what she owes after that Payment. So the wording can't be fixed when the Notice is made.

Once it's sent, it's the opposite. Sunita received a particular message on a particular day. If she rings next week and says "your message said ₹35,723", the Lender needs to see exactly that message, not one rewritten with today's figures. A log that rewrites itself is not a log.

And the Lender's actions need guarding. Sending a Notice twice, or approving one that has already gone, would make the log lie.

The idea

Until a Notice is sent or skipped, its row is only a Customer and a date. Which Mortgages it covers, the amounts and the wording are all worked out whenever it is read, from the replay (ADR 0001). The moment it is sent or skipped, the app freezes it: it stores the message and the list of Mortgages as they were at that instant.

Each action is allowed only from certain statuses. Anything else is refused with a 409.

The flow

Notice there's no arrow from awaiting_approval straight to sent. A held Notice has to be approved first.

The code

1. What each action needs, and what it makes

The whole state diagram is this one table. Every action looks up its row: the set of statuses it may start from, and the status it ends in. To add an action, you'd add a row here.

2. act: check, freeze, update

  1. act reads the Notice through the same list_notices the page uses. So the message it freezes is exactly the one the Lender was looking at.
  2. A Notice can lose everything it had to say: its Mortgage was Redeemed, or became Dormant, after the Notice was made. Sending an empty message is refused; skipping it is fine.
  3. Freezing happens for both sent and skipped. A skipped Notice keeps the message the Lender decided not to send, so the log shows what was skipped.

The routes (lines 438–451) are POST /api/notices/{id}/approve, /skip and /sent. All three call _answer (line 426), which runs act in engine.begin() and turns NoticeNotFound into a 404 and NoticeRefused into a 409. Note the route is /sent, past tense: it records that the Lender has sent it (below).

3. Worded on read, or kept

  1. Only unsent rows are passed to _accruing, so only they get an entry in accruing. Every other row falls to line 303 and shows what was stored. The check-yourself exercise breaks exactly this line.

4. Which Mortgages, and which figures

  1. A Projection (chapter 004, lesson 5) replays the Mortgage forward to a future day, assuming no more Payments. [then] = … unpacks a one-item list, and fails loudly if there isn't exactly one. The figures are 004's; this function only picks two of them.
  2. The Notice row doesn't say which Mortgages it covers. They're found again on every read: the Customer's Active Mortgages that accrue on accrues_on and aren't Dormant then. A Mortgage added after the Notice was made, accruing the same day, joins it (test_a_queued_notice_covers_a_mortgage_added_later).

The Dashboard's counts use unsent_counts (line 213). It runs the same _accruing and counts only Notices that still have a Mortgage to talk about, without wording any. So a Notice with nothing to say doesn't show up as work to do.

Nuances

(a) Pay first, then look

Predict: on a fresh copy, Sunita pays the same ₹5,000 before the Notice is approved. What do "Interest added" and "Owed after" say now?

Interest added₹700₹700

From scripts/run_throughs.py (section L4), on a copy: GET /api/notices?status=awaiting_approval.

The unsent Notice was worded again on read, so it shows the new Outstanding. The interest stays ₹700: the month from 21 Sep is charged on the Principal at its start, ₹35,000 (trap #7, ADR 0006). A Notice quotes the month's interest even when some of it was paid ahead (test_interest_paid_ahead_is_still_the_months_interest_added).

(b) Skip instead

On a copy, POST /api/notices/58/skip straight from awaiting_approval gives status skipped, approved 0, the 239-character message stored, notice_mortgage (58, 9), and sent_at NULL. Approving it afterwards is a 409: "This Notice is already skipped". (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section L4.)

The row is still there, because sync only removes Notices that are too far ahead. But _accruing finds no Active Mortgage of Sunita's accruing on 21 Oct, so there's nothing to say, and only skipping finishes it.

(d) "Sent" means "the Lender opened WhatsApp"

The app never talks to WhatsApp's servers. On the Notices page, the Send button opens WhatsApp with the message ready, and at the same moment tells the backend it was sent (WhatsApp.tsx calls sender.send, then onSent; chapter 012). The Lender still has to press Send in WhatsApp. If they close it instead, the app already says "sent". That's the price of click-to-send, which ADR 0002 chose over automating WhatsApp Web. Lesson 5 shows the link.

(e) A template edit only rewords unsent Notices

On a copy, the Lender saves the Notice template Hello {customer_name}: {interest} on {date}.. #58 now reads "Hello Sunita Rao: ₹700 on 21 Oct 2026." Sent Notice #57, to Arjun, still begins "*Interest notice* 🔔 … Namaste Arjun Patel": its text was frozen when it was sent. (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section L4.)

Quiz

A Notice is queued. What does the notice row hold in its message column?

Only act writes message, and only when the Notice becomes sent or skipped (line 387). Until then the API words it on every read, from the replay and the current template.

"Approve first" is on. Which request moves an awaiting_approval Notice to sent?

Send needs queued (_ACTIONS). /sent on an awaiting Notice is a 409. Skipped is final, so nothing can follow a skip.

Which table tells you, months later, which Mortgages a sent Notice talked about?

notice has no Mortgage column: a Notice is a Customer and a date. While unsent, its Mortgages are worked out; once sent or skipped, act writes one notice_mortgage row per Mortgage, and list_notices reads those.
