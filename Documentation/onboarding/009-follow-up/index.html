Chapter 009

Follow-up

The problem this chapter solves

Lending is half the job. The other half is getting the money back. That means keeping in touch with Customers. Each morning the Lender wants two answers. Who should I phone today? Who should I tell, before it happens, that interest is about to be added?

Both answers change every day, and with every Payment. Arjun hasn't paid for his ruby ring in fifteen months, so he should be phoned. If he pays this afternoon, he shouldn't be. Sunita's mangalsutra adds ₹700 of interest on 21 Oct, so she should get a message two days ahead. If she pays something first, the message's figures are out of date. And once a message has gone out, the app must remember what it actually said, even if the figures change later.

None of this can be stored and forgotten. A list typed up on Monday is wrong by Tuesday. And the app can't send anything itself: the Lender sends every message from their own WhatsApp. This chapter shows how the app works out who to phone and who to message, how it keeps those lists current without a scheduler, and how a message is built and handed to WhatsApp.

What you'll be able to do

  • Say why any Mortgage is or isn't on today's Call List, and where it sorts.
  • Record a Call Note one at a time or from a Call Sheet, and say why an imported row was left out.
  • Say which unsent Notices should exist today, and when they are worked out again.
  • Follow a Notice from awaiting approval to sent or skipped, and say what is stored at each step.
  • Change a message template safely, and explain how a message reaches WhatsApp.

Before you start

Chapter 001, lesson 5 walked through the Lender's morning in plain words: the Call List, Call Notes, Call Sheets and Notices. This chapter opens the code behind each. You need three ideas from earlier chapters:

  • Months Unpaid and the Projection come from the Ledger engine (chapter 004). This chapter only compares their results with the Lender's limits.
  • Outstanding and Dormant come from chapter 005.
  • Loan-to-Value comes from chapter 008, lesson 5. On 19 Oct, Arjun's ruby ring is at 104.9%.

You'll also see what a Receipt says from chapter 007, lesson 5. Lesson 5 here shows how that text is laid out.

Keep the sample book served from chapter 002, lesson 4 (http://127.0.0.1:8765, held on 19 Oct 2026). Build it fresh into a new, empty folder and set $BOOK to that folder, because chapter 008 saved changes on the old one. This chapter saves changes too, all through the API: a Call Note imported from a Call Sheet in lesson 2, the "approve first" setting turned off and back on in lesson 3, Sunita's Notice approved and sent and a Payment in lesson 4, and a template in lesson 5. Each lesson says what it changes. Never try any of it against real data.

You'll read the database with sqlite3 $BOOK/data/mortgage.db. Only read it; every write goes through the API.

The map

Notice the difference between the halves. The Call List has no table at all. Only its two limits and the Call Notes are stored. Notices do have a table, because the Lender acts on each one (approves, sends, skips) and the app has to remember that. Even so, an unsent Notice row holds only a Customer and a date. Its message is worked out every time it's read.
