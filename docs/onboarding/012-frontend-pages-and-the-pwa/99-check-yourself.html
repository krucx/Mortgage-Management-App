Chapter 012 · Check yourself

Check yourself: Frontend pages and the PWA

Questions from all six lessons, mixed up. Then make the update banner reload only for the exact version it offered, and see which test catches it.

Quiz

1. A bug report says: "the screen at /mortgages/4/redeem shows the wrong button label". Which file draws the button?

App.tsx maps /mortgages/:id/redeem to RedeemPage, a read page that hands the Mortgage to CloseMortgage with initialAction="redeem". The button ("Redeem for ₹…") is drawn there. (Lessons 1 and 3.)

2. On the served book, with your browser on 3 Nov 2026, the Payment form refuses to list Meera's Mortgages. Why?

todayIso() is the browser's date, so the form asks for the Outstanding as_of=2026-11-03, and the backend, whose today is 19 Oct, answers 422 "The date cannot be in the future". max is the browser's today too (3 Nov), and Payments can be on any day. (Lesson 1, nuance d.)

3. Meera's Receipt for 10 Sept says "Still owed ₹81,253" for the chain. Where does that figure come from?

The page shows formatINR(line.owed_after); the backend worked it out by replaying the chain to just after that Payment. Pages show figures and never compute money. (Lesson 1.)

4. Why is ChangeTermsForm rendered as <ChangeTermsForm key={id} …/>?

A new key makes React draw a new component, whose useState starts from the new Mortgage. The script typed 1.25 on the bangles, went to the chain's form, and found 2 there. Without the key the bangles' draft could be saved for the chain. (Lesson 2, nuance b.)

5. You stop the server and reload the installed app. Which cache answers the page, and what does it show?

Pages are NetworkFirst: the server is asked, fails, and the pages cache (or /index.html, for a page never visited) answers. The app then loads from the precache, and StartGate's ping fails: "The app isn't running…". No /api answer is ever cached. (Lesson 6.)

6. The Lender presses Later on version 1.6.0. What does the app remember, and where?

writePutOff stores the version under mortgage-management.update-put-off in localStorage. The banner stays hidden until a release newer than 1.6.0 is offered. Each device decides for itself. (Lesson 6, nuance d.)

7. The banner offered 1.6.0, but Setup installed 1.6.1, released in the meantime. Why does the app still reload?

Line 137: data.running !== status.running. Any version other than the one running when Install now was pressed means the update worked. The same version coming back means it failed. (Lesson 6, nuance e; the exercise below.)

8. Trace it: on a fresh book on 19 Oct, Meera pays ₹2,00,000 equally across the bangles (owes ₹1,51,500) and the chain (₹83,333). What does the form suggest, and will the Preview Redeem anything?

The ₹1,00,000 share is more than the chain's ₹83,333, so allocateEqually gives the chain exactly its Outstanding and the bangles the rest (the script printed [1,16,667, 83,333]). An Allocation of exactly the Outstanding Redeems the chain (trap #3), so the confirmation becomes "Redeem and hand back the jewellery?". (Lesson 3.)

9. The Dashboard's total Loan-to-Value was 36.3% on a fresh book, and 38.1% after lesson 3. Why did it go up?

Only Active, valued Mortgages count. The anklet owed ₹0 but was valued at ₹54,144, so it lowered the ratio; once Redeemed, the same Outstanding is divided by ₹10,55,808 instead of ₹11,09,952. (Lessons 3 and 5.)

Hands-on: reload only for the version offered

Lesson 6 said the app reloads for whichever new version runs, even one newer than the banner offered. Here you narrow that to "only the version offered" and see what reminders. Work in frontend/.

    } else if (data.running === available.version) {

Recap

  • App.tsx maps every URL to one page in src/pages/, inside StartGate and one shell (header, Nav with the Dormant dot from GET /api/closures/dormant, ThemeToggle, UpdateBanner). A read page asks with a read from requests.ts, shows "Loading…" or a status error, then draws with the shared CSS classes, formatINR (paise, Indian grouping) and formatDate (UTC, never shifted). Pages never compute money. Customer-facing text names jewellery by its description. The theme follows the browser unless chosen, kept in localStorage. todayIso() is the browser's today; the backend decides what's refused (lesson 1).
  • A form page loads, then renders its form with key={id}. The form keeps a draft, works out the exact change (or null), and gives it to useProposedChange with a Preview, a Summary confirmation, a save and a navigate. PreviewSection and BeforeAfter draw any Preview: Owed today, interest over 12 months, the Outstanding at 3, 6 and 12 months, the chart and the changed Ledger steps (lesson 2).
  • The Payment form suggests equal whole-rupee shares, each capped at that Mortgage's Outstanding, leftovers to the first; more than all is owed is split uncapped for the Preview to refuse. A Redemption is read from the Preview's after.closure. useClosing previews and saves Redeem, Waive off and Forfeit for one or several Mortgages, all or none, reading each Closure's figures from the Preview (lesson 3).
  • The Mortgage page is a list of panels; one that saves calls setMortgage, which updates the cache and marks History stale. The Ledger shows 8 steps, History 5 grouped changes, the rest in a ScrollPopup. ID numbers are read in the browser by Tesseract, from the app's own /ocr/, into empty fields only. useSaveWithPhotos saves the record once, then each photo; a retry sends only what's left (lesson 4).
  • The Call List labels the backend's reasons; MarkCalled saves a Call Note with useChange. WhatsAppPreview draws *bold*, _italic_, ~strike~ and ``` boxes; SendOnWhatsApp opens WhatsApp through sender and then marks the Reminder sent (ADR 0002). Settings is one section per group. Every Dashboard figure is one GET /api/dashboard, each tile a link to its list; the total Loan-to-Value counts only valued Active Mortgages (lesson 5).
  • The service worker precaches the built app (not ocr/), fetches pages NetworkFirst with a 5 s timeout and falls back to the cached index.html, and never caches /api. StartGate shows the app only once /api/payment-methods answers below 500, checks every 2 s, and reloads. UpdateBanner offers /api/update's release; Later is per device; it reloads for any version other than the one running before. The server's cache headers and service worker's caching rules both affect updates (lesson 6).

The frontend sheet now lists every page and the routes it calls. The commands sheet has building and installing the app from serve.py; the traps sheet records why the old two-server cache mismatch was retired.
