Chapter 009 · Check yourself

Check yourself: Follow-up

Questions from all five lessons, mixed up. Each is about a fresh sample book, before any of this chapter's saves. Then one small break in the real code, which shows how a log can quietly stop being a log.

Quiz

1. Nobody pays anything. Served on 19 Nov 2026, is Arjun's gold chain (started 19 Aug, 2 Months Unpaid on 19 Oct) on the Call List, and why?

On 19 Nov the chain adds its third month's interest with none paid, so Months Unpaid reaches the limit of 3 (>=). It also accrues on the 19th. One row carries both reasons. Loan-to-Value is only one of three reasons. (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section 99: 3 Months Unpaid, 29.4%. Lesson 1.)

2. On 21 Oct, nobody has called anyone yet. Where does Arjun's ruby ring sort?

"Called" means a Call Note dated today (_todays_notes). The 19 Oct note doesn't count on 21 Oct, so the ring is uncalled, and 15 Months Unpaid beats Sunita's 1. (Lesson 1.)

3. The app isn't opened after 19 Oct until 23 Oct. Days ahead is 2. Which unsent Reminders exist that morning?

The first request on 23 Oct syncs: 25 Oct is within 2 days, so Meera's Reminder is made, created_on 23 Oct. Sunita's was made on 19 Oct and is still unsent. sync never removes a Reminder for a past date; the Lender skips it. (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section L3 (c2). Lesson 3.)

4. A sheet comes back with a note for 2026-Sunita-Mangalsutra, Called on 20 Sep 2026. What does the import say?

The mangalsutra started on 21 Sep. left_out_because checks both ends: not after today, not before the start. A left-out row is named with its row number and reason; only empty notes and duplicates are just counted. (Lesson 2.)

5. A queued Reminder is skipped. What does its row hold afterwards?

act stores the message and the reminder_mortgage rows for any Reminder leaving the unsent statuses, sent or skipped. Only sending sets sent_at. (Lesson 4.)

6. Which template saves?

{reference} is a Receipt placeholder; a Reminder has no Payment to refer to. { total_owed } with spaces isn't exactly a placeholder, so it's refused too. The Receipt template uses only its own two placeholders. (From scripts/run_throughs.py, section 99. Lesson 5.)

7. Which of these requests can write to the reminder table?

The Dashboard syncs the Reminders first, inside engine.begin(), so its counts agree with the Reminders page. The Call List reads only. A Payment doesn't sync: the next read will (though, as the first request of a new day, any request runs the daily jobs). (Lesson 3.)

8. Sunita's Reminder was sent on 19 Oct. On 20 Oct the Lender corrects her mangalsutra's Principal. What does the sent Reminder say now?

A sent Reminder shows the stored message; only unsent ones are worded on read (list_reminders, line 292). That's the next exercise. (Lesson 4.)

Recap

  • The Call List is worked out on request; only its two limits and the Call Notes are stored. An Active, non-Dormant Mortgage is listed for Months Unpaid at or above the limit (3), Loan-to-Value strictly above it (85%), or accruing today, and can carry several reasons. Rows sort uncalled first, then most Months Unpaid, then highest Loan-to-Value, grouped per Customer. "Called" means a Call Note today (lesson 1, ADR 0012).
  • Call Notes follow one set of rules either way (left_out_because): Active, not empty, not after today, not before the start. One added on its own is dated today; an imported one takes the sheet's Called on. An exact duplicate is kept once, so importing twice adds nothing. Left-out rows are named, empty ones and duplicates counted. Call Notes aren't History (lesson 2).
  • There's no scheduler. sync makes the unsent Reminders what they should be (one per Customer per coming Accrual Date within the days ahead, none for Dormant Mortgages), and is safe to repeat. It runs in the daily run, on every read of the Reminders or the Dashboard, and on saving message settings. Missed days are made up only for dates still to come; approvals are forgotten while "approve first" is off (lesson 3, trap #8).
  • An unsent Reminder is a Customer and a date; its Mortgages, figures and wording are worked out on every read. Sending or skipping freezes the message and reminder_mortgage. Approve needs awaiting, send needs queued, skip needs unsent; anything else is a 409. A Reminder with nothing to say can only be skipped. "Sent" means the Lender opened WhatsApp (lesson 4, ADR 0001, ADR 0002).
  • The backend builds every message: cards per Mortgage in a monospace block, at least 24 wide, titled by description, totals only for two or more. Each template has its own placeholders; any other brace is refused at save. The frontend hands the text to sender, which opens api.whatsapp.com/send with 91 added, never wa.me (lesson 5, trap #16).
