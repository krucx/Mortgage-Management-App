Chapter 001

What the app does, in plain words

The problem this app solves

A Lender lends money to local families against pledged gold jewellery. Each packet of jewellery sits in the Lender's safe while interest builds up month by month, Payments come in, and eventually the jewellery goes home or is sold. For years this lived in a paper book, where one wrong sum or one late entry quietly spoils every figure after it, and nobody can say quickly what a family owes today.

This app is that book, on the Lender's own laptop. Before you read a line of its code, you need the business it models: the people, the money, and the rules. Every name in the code comes from this chapter's words. This chapter has no code at all. It's the part a developer usually picks up by asking colleagues for weeks.

What you'll be able to do

  • Say who lends what to whom, against what, and on which Terms.
  • Work out a month's interest from a Mortgage's Terms, and what it owes today.
  • Say what one Payment does to one or more Mortgages, and how a mistake is put right.
  • Name each way a Mortgage ends, and tell a Dormant Mortgage from a closed one.
  • Describe the Lender's morning, and explain why changing an old Payment moves today's figures.

Before you start

Nothing: this is the first chapter. You don't need to install or run anything. Keep The Gold Chain Story open in another tab. It tells the same year of the same sample book on the app's real screens, and each lesson points to the part where you can check its figures.
