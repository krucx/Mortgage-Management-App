Chapter 008 · Lesson 3

Adding, editing and deleting a Customer

You've met the rules a Customer's details must follow. This lesson shows where they run: the three change functions in customer_changes.py, the History they leave, and the small Previews the Customer form uses to ask "would this save?" before it saves.

By the end you can follow a Customer change through refuse → write → History, and use its 204 Previews.

The problem

A Customer's details change. Meera moves house. The Lender types her PAN wrong and fixes it. A Customer added by mistake has to go. Each of these needs the same care as a change to a Mortgage (chapter 006): refuse anything that can't stand, write it in one transaction, and leave a record in History of what changed.

Customers also carry two pieces of official ID that need care of their own. India's Aadhaar Number is a 12-digit national identity number; leaking it is serious, and the government issues a "masked" copy showing only the last 4 digits for exactly this reason. A PAN (Permanent Account Number) is a 10-character tax identifier, and each person has one. The app must never keep a full Aadhaar number, and must never let two Customers share a PAN.

And deleting is dangerous. A Customer with Mortgages has a Ledger, Payments and Receipts that all point at them. Deleting them would orphan all of that.

The idea

Each change to a Customer is one function in people/customer_changes.py that refuses, writes, then records History, the same shape as lending/changes.py. The save routes call it inside engine.begin(). Each Preview route calls the very same function inside engine.connect() and rolls back, then answers 204 No Content: no body, because the only question it answers is "would this save?". A Customer has no Ledger, so there are no before/after figures to show.

The flow

Notice the form (CustomerFormPage) sends the same body twice: once to check as the Lender types, once to save. That's why its Preview is refused exactly when saving would be (trap #5, owned by chapter 006).

The code

1. What a Customer body may hold: the ID checks

Like Phone in lesson 1, these are wrapped as Pydantic types (AadhaarLast4, Pan, Email, line 87) and used on CustomerIn (line 118). So a full Aadhaar number never gets past request parsing: by the time create_customer runs, only 4 digits are left. tidy_email (line 56) does the same for an email: trimmed, roughly checked, and empty means None.

2. Create and edit

Lessons 1 and 2 showed _checked's Primary Number and Referrer rules. Its one remaining check is the PAN, at lines 187–194: if another Customer already holds this PAN, it raises 409 via _pan_taken. Then:

  1. Every refusal is in _checked. Nothing has been written when it raises.
  2. A Customer has no Ledger, so there's no check_replays step after the write, unlike lending/changes.py.
  3. audit.created and audit.changes (chapter 006, lesson 3) turn the before and after into one row per field that differs. An edit that changes nothing records nothing.

3. What History sees: _AUDITED and _audited

History compares what the Customer page shows, not the raw columns. The Referrer is recorded by name, so History reads "Ravi Verma → Arjun Patel", not "referrer 1 → customer 2". Photos aren't in the list: they get their own entries (lesson 4).

4. Delete

  1. Anyone this Customer referred keeps their record, with no Referrer.
  2. The name goes in note, because the row it would describe is gone.
  3. The photo files to delete once the change is saved: lesson 4.

"Any Mortgage" includes closed ones. A Customer whose every Mortgage was Redeemed years ago still can't be deleted, because their Payments and Receipts point at them.

5. The routes and their Previews

There are three Customer Previews, each beside its save route: POST /customers/preview, PUT /customers/{id}/preview (line 345) and POST /customers/{id}/delete-preview (line 415). Unlike the Mortgage Previews in lending/previews.py, these live in the same file as the saves, because they're three short functions with nothing to compare.

Nuances

(a) A PUT that leaves fields out

Predict: the same PUT, but the body leaves out email and referrer entirely. Refused, ignored, or something else?

From scripts/run_throughs.py (section L3), on a fresh copy, where Meera's Referrer is still Standalone Referrer Ravi.

Both are cleared. CustomerIn gives every optional field a default of None, and edit_customer writes every field. PUT here means "this is the Customer now", not "change these fields". The Customer form (CustomerFormPage) sends every field, so it never trips on this; a script or a new page could.

(b) History keeps the Referrer by name

On a fresh copy, changing Meera's Referrer from Standalone Referrer Ravi to Customer Arjun records referrer: "Ravi Verma" → "Arjun Patel". That's readable months later, even if an id would mean nothing by then. It's also why lesson 2's promotion recorded nothing: by name, Meera's Referrer never changed.

Chapter 011 covers how the frontend calls these.

(d) Deleting a Referrer Customer

Predict: Asha (no Mortgages) referred Bina. Asha is deleted. What is Bina's Referrer now, and what does Asha's History row say?

Bina has no Referrer, and Asha's History is one deleted row with note "Asha Rao" and no field. Bina's record stays; only the link goes. Like a promotion, the unlinking writes nothing in Bina's History (from scripts/run_throughs.py, section L3).

Rule: refuse in one place, write in one place. Every rule about a Customer (Primary Number, own Referrer, PAN, Mortgages before delete) is in customer_changes.py, and every route only parses, calls and answers. When you add a rule, add it to _checked or the change function, and the Preview gets it for free.

Quiz

What does POST /api/customers/preview return for a Customer that would save?

It answers only "would this save?". There's no Ledger to compare, so no body. The real 201 with the Customer comes from POST /api/customers.

The Lender types a whole Aadhaar number, 1234 5678 9012. Where are the first 8 digits dropped?

AadhaarLast4 runs tidy_aadhaar as Pydantic reads the body, returning digits[-4:]. No change function ever sees the full number, and the column has no length limit.

Kavita's coin was Redeemed on 5 Oct; her anklet is Active. Can Kavita be deleted once the anklet is Redeemed too?

remove_customer looks for any mortgage row with her customer_id, whatever its status. Closed Mortgages keep their Payments and Receipts, which still point at her.
