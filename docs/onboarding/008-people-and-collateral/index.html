Chapter 008

People and Collateral

The problem this chapter solves

Every Mortgage belongs to a person, and every Mortgage is backed by jewellery. Both sound simple, and neither is.

People first. Two Customers can have the same name. One family can share a phone. A neighbour who brought a Customer in, and isn't a Customer, can turn up a year later wanting to borrow. Then the app has to know it is the same person, and not keep two records that drift apart. Someone types Meera's number as "+91 98100-12345" one day and "9810012345" the next. The app also keeps photos: a picture of each Customer, and photos of their Aadhaar and PAN cards. Those are files on disk, outside the database, and they must never get out of step with it.

Then the jewellery. The Lender lent ₹60,000 against Arjun's ruby ring. Is that still safe? It depends on what the gold is worth today, and gold prices move. The Lender needs a figure for what each piece is worth, and how much of that is already owed, so that a Mortgage owing more than its gold is worth gets a phone call.

This chapter is about how the app decides who a person is, how it links the people who introduce Customers, how it changes a Customer and their photos safely, and how it values the jewellery.

What you'll be able to do

  • Say why a phone number, not a name, identifies a person, and point to the line of code that enforces it.
  • Take any Referrer choice in a request and say which row it writes, or which existing person it links to.
  • Follow a Customer change through refuse → write → History, and use its 204 Previews.
  • Add a change that writes a photo file, keeping the file and the database consistent.
  • Hand-work any Mortgage's Collateral Value and Loan-to-Value.

Before you start

Chapter 001, lesson 1 introduced Customers, Referrers and the Lender in plain words. Chapter 005, lesson 1 taught Outstanding, what would Redeem a Mortgage today; this chapter divides it by the jewellery's value. Chapter 006 taught the shape every change has (refuse → write → check → History) and how a Preview runs the real change and rolls it back. Customer changes follow the same shape, so this chapter shows it again without re-teaching it.

Keep the sample book served from chapter 002, lesson 4 (http://127.0.0.1:8765, held on 19 Oct 2026). Build it fresh into a new empty folder and set $BOOK to that folder: the Payment you saved in chapter 007 is still on the old one. This chapter saves changes on it: two new Customers in lesson 2, an edit in lesson 3, a photo in lesson 4 and a Collateral Value in lesson 5. Each lesson says what it changes. Never try any of it against real data.

You'll read the database with sqlite3 ($BOOK/data/mortgage.db) and list the photos folder ($BOOK/data/photos). Only read them; every write goes through the API.

The map

Notice the two stores on the right. People are rows in the database; photos are files next to it. Lesson 4 is about keeping the two in step. And notice Collateral Value has no table of its own: like the Ledger, it is worked out each time it is asked for.
