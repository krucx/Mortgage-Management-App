Reference sheet

Glossary

The words this codebase uses for its business, copied word for word from GLOSSARY.md, which is the canonical source. Each term links to the lesson that teaches it.

The code, the screens, the tests and the issues all use these words with exactly these meanings. When you write code or an issue, use them too, and never a word from a term's Avoid list: "Outstanding", not "balance"; "Customer", not "borrower". This sheet grows as chapters are written; it holds the terms taught so far. How the glossary is laid out, and how to get from a term to the ADR behind it, is 002 · Lesson 3.

People

Lender
The single person who runs the app and lends money against pledged jewellery. Avoid: Loaner, loanee, user, admin Taught in 001 · Lesson 1
Customer
A person who has borrowed from the Lender by pledging jewellery. Avoid: Borrower, loanee, client Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 008 · Lesson 3
Referrer
A person who introduced one or more Customers to the Lender; either an existing Customer or a person known only as a Referrer. Avoid: Reference Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 008 · Lesson 2
Standalone Referrer
A Referrer who is not a Customer, known only by name, address and phone. Avoid: External referrer, contact Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 008 · Lesson 2
Primary Number
The 10-digit mobile number that identifies a person: a Customer's contact number 1 or a Standalone Referrer's phone. No two people share one. Avoid: Customer ID, main phone Taught in 008 · Lesson 1
Aadhaar Number
The last 4 digits of a Customer's Aadhaar, as their masked Aadhaar shows them; the full number is never kept. Avoid: UID, Aadhaar ID Taught in 008 · Lesson 3
PAN
A Customer's Permanent Account Number, which no two Customers share. Avoid: PAN number, tax ID Taught in 008 · Lesson 3
ID Photos
Photos of a Customer's Aadhaar card (the masked front) and PAN card, kept on the Customer and shown only on their page and form. Avoid: KYC documents, ID proof Taught in 008 · Lesson 4

Lending

Mortgage
One loan against one pledged packet of jewellery, which may contain several pieces described together. Avoid: Loan, pledge, item Taught in 001 · Lesson 1
Mortgage Name
A human-readable label for a Mortgage, beginning with the year it started. Avoid: Pledge number, mortgage ID Taught in 001 · Lesson 1
Terms
A Mortgage's rate, Interest Mode and Part-Month Rule, set when it is created. Its starting Terms can be corrected by editing the Mortgage, replaying existing Payments; a Term Change applies new Terms from its dated Accrual Date onward. Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 004 · Lesson 2, 006 · Lesson 2
Default Terms
The Terms the Lender has set to be copied into every new Mortgage. Avoid: Global rate, settings Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 010 · Lesson 3
Lent Through
How a Mortgage's Principal was handed to the Customer: Cash, UPI or bank transfer. Avoid: Disbursal mode, payout Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 007 · Lesson 4
Term Change
A dated change to some of a Mortgage's Terms, starting on an Accrual Date and applying to the months from then on; it keeps earlier interest under its prior Terms. Correct the Mortgage's starting Terms by editing the Mortgage, which replays its existing Payments. Avoid: Rate edit, amendment Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 004 · Lesson 5, 016 · Lesson 1 In the code: 006 · Lesson 1
Principal
The amount on a Mortgage on which interest is charged; grows when unpaid interest is capitalised and shrinks when payments exceed interest. Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 004 · Lesson 3
Accrual Date
The monthly day on which a month's interest is added, charged on the Principal as it stood when that month began, at the previous Accrual Date before that day's Payments; taken from the start date and clamped to month-end when the month is shorter. Avoid: Due date, interest date Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 004 · Lesson 2
Interest Mode
How a Mortgage's interest grows: Simple, Compounded Yearly (simple monthly, capitalised every year), or Compounded Monthly. Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 004 · Lesson 4
Capitalisation
Moving unpaid interest into Principal at the end of a compounding period. Avoid: Compounding event, rollover Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 004 · Lesson 4
Part-Month Rule
How the Interest to Date is charged for a month under way: pro-rata (each day started, out of 30), full month (the whole month once it starts), or nothing. Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 004 · Lesson 2
Interest to Date
The interest for the month under way, as of a date, under its Part-Month Rule: counted from its first day (the start or an Accrual Date) through that date, so today is always charged; it changes from day to day until the month's interest is charged on the next Accrual Date, and is fixed at a Closure. Avoid: Accrued interest, part-month charge, pending interest Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 004 · Lesson 3
Ledger
The step-by-step history of a Mortgage — interest added, capitalisations, payments — derived from its terms and payments. Avoid: Statement, MortgageLedger Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 003 · Lesson 4, 004 · Lesson 1
Replay
Working out a Mortgage's Ledger or Outstanding from its stored facts — its Terms, Term Changes, Closure and the Allocations of its non-Voided Payments — rather than storing them (ADR 0001). Avoid: Recalculation, recompute, refresh Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 003 · Lesson 4, 004 · Lesson 1, 005 · Lesson 1
Projection
The Ledger continued into the future under stated assumptions, used to compare outcomes before a decision. Avoid: Forecast, estimate Taught in 004 · Lesson 5
Preview
What a change would do, shown before it is saved: the change is made and then undone, so a Preview is refused exactly when saving would be. Avoid: Dry run, simulation Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 003 · Lesson 2 In the code: 006 · Lesson 4, 011 · Lesson 4 (the screen's side)
Simulation
A made-up Mortgage (an amount, a start date, Terms and perhaps planned Payments: a monthly amount and Lump Sums) worked out exactly as a real one's Ledger would be, to size up a loan before lending; it has no Customer, only perhaps a jewellery value for its Loan-to-Value, and is never kept. Avoid: Quote, estimate, dummy mortgage In the code: 005 · Lesson 6
Lump Sum
A one-off planned Payment in a Simulation: an amount on a date, paid as a real Payment would be, beside any monthly amount paid on every Accrual Date. Avoid: One-time payment, prepayment, part payment In the code: 005 · Lesson 6
Trend
The monthly average of the last 3 full months (Payments received, Principal lent, Redemptions) carried forward as an estimate; nothing is replayed, so unlike a Projection it is never exact. Avoid: Forecast, prediction Named in 004 · Lesson 5
Outstanding
What a Mortgage owes on a date: Principal plus unpaid interest plus its Interest to Date, so exactly what would redeem it that day; nothing once closed. Avoid: Redemption Amount, balance, dues, payoff Taught in 001 · Lesson 2 In the code: 005 · Lesson 1

Collateral

Gold Rate
The Lender's current price per gram of gold of one purity. Avoid: Market rate, price Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 008 · Lesson 5
Collateral Value
The current estimated worth of a Mortgage's jewellery: its net weight at the Gold Rate for its purity, unless the Lender sets a value by hand. Avoid: Product value, current_product_value Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 006 · Lesson 5 In the code: 008 · Lesson 5
Loan-to-Value
A Mortgage's Outstanding as a share of its Collateral Value. Avoid: LTV ratio, coverage Taught in 001 · Lesson 1 In the code: 008 · Lesson 5

Payments

Payment
A single sum received from a Customer on one date, possibly covering several Mortgages; any Final Payments in it are kept as parts of it, sharing its one Receipt. Avoid: Receipt (that is the acknowledgement of a Payment), collection Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 003 · Lesson 3, 007 · Lesson 1, 007 · Lesson 2
Final Payment
What a Mortgage is paid as it is Redeemed or Waived: kept as its own part of the Payment it came in, so a Reopen Voids it alone and leaves the rest. Avoid: Settlement, closing payment Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 3, 007 · Lesson 2
Receipt
The acknowledgement of a Payment for the Customer: what each Mortgage was paid and still owes (or that it was Redeemed), sent on WhatsApp or printed; it names each Mortgage by its description, never its Mortgage Name. Avoid: Invoice, bill Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 003 · Lesson 5, 007 · Lesson 5
Allocation
The portion of a Payment applied to one Mortgage; within a Mortgage it pays unpaid interest, then the Interest to Date (paid ahead of that month's interest), then Principal. Avoid: Split, contribution Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 003 · Lesson 4, 004 · Lesson 3, 007 · Lesson 1
Discount
A non-cash reduction to what a Customer owes on one Mortgage, recorded alongside that Mortgage's cash Allocation in a Payment. Cash is applied first; the Discount then pays unpaid interest, the Interest to Date, and Principal, in that order. It can contribute to Redeeming the Mortgage. Voiding the Payment, including when Reopen undoes a Redemption, reverses both the cash and Discount. Avoid: Waiver (a Discount does not itself close a Mortgage) Taught in 007 · Lesson 1 In the code: 012 · Lesson 3
Cash Limit
The amount at or above which the Lender is warned before lending or receiving it in cash, since Government of India rules ask for UPI or bank transfer instead; each Payment is one sum (its Final Payments included), and so is each Mortgage's Principal lent. Avoid: Cash cap, cash ceiling Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 007 · Lesson 4, 011 · Lesson 4 (the warning in Save)
Void
Cancelling a recorded Payment with a reason, keeping it on record but excluding it from every Ledger. Avoid: Delete, reverse Taught in 001 · Lesson 3 In the code: 005 · Lesson 5, 007 · Lesson 3

Follow-up

Notice
A message to a Customer, sent ahead of Accrual Dates, covering every one of their Mortgages accruing on that day; queued by the daily run, then approved (if the Lender chose to), sent or skipped. Avoid: Reminder, notification Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 009 · Lesson 3, 009 · Lesson 4, 009 · Lesson 5
Months Unpaid
How many of a Mortgage's latest monthly interest charges (never its Interest to Date) its Payments have not yet covered, counting Payments against the oldest interest first and interest paid ahead against its own month; capitalised interest still counts as unpaid. Avoid: Months overdue, arrears Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 004 · Lesson 3, 009 · Lesson 1
Call List
The day's Mortgages the Lender should phone about: too many Months Unpaid, Loan-to-Value too high, or accruing today. Avoid: Due list, payments due Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 009 · Lesson 1
Call Note
The Lender's record of phoning a Customer about a Mortgage, with what was said. Avoid: Follow-up, contact log Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 009 · Lesson 2
Call Sheet
The day's Call List as a printable or Excel sheet, a row per Mortgage, for whoever makes the calls to write each Call Note on; the Excel sheet is imported back to record them all at once. Avoid: Export, call report Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 009 · Lesson 2

Safekeeping

History
The record of every change to a Customer, Mortgage or Payment — when, what, and the old and new values — which is never edited or removed. Avoid: Log, changelog, activity Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 In the code: 003 · Lesson 3 In the code: 006 · Lesson 3
Backup
A copy of all the Lender's data — the database and photos — kept in a folder outside the app, from which everything can be restored. Avoid: Export, snapshot, archive Taught in 001 · Lesson 5 When one is taken: 010 · Lesson 2 (weekly), 010 · Lesson 5 (before an update)

Status

Active
A Mortgage still accruing interest with jewellery held. Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 5
Dormant
An Active Mortgage that owes nothing, so its jewellery should already have gone back: usually a recording slip, put right by Redeeming it. Avoid: Ready to Redeem, paid up Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 5
Redeemed
A Mortgage fully repaid with its jewellery returned to the Customer. Avoid: Settled, paid off Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 3
Closure
The end of a Mortgage, by Redemption, Waiver or Forfeiture; after it no interest accrues and no Payments are taken, unless it is Reopened. Avoid: Closing out, termination Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 2
Waived
A Mortgage closed with the jewellery returned although the Customer's final Payment fell short of its Outstanding on the Closure date (or there was none): the Lender, at their discretion, lets off the rest, the Waived Amount. Avoid: Written off, settled, discounted Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 4
Forfeited
A Mortgage on which the Customer defaulted and the Lender kept or sold the jewellery. Avoid: Defaulted, auctioned Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 4
Reopen
Undoing a Closure made by mistake, with a reason: the Mortgage is Active again as if it had never been closed, and a Redemption's or Waiver's final Payment is Voided. Avoid: Undo, unclose, revive Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 5
Recovered Amount
What a Forfeited Mortgage's jewellery fetched (or is worth, if kept); its difference from the Outstanding on the Closure date is the Lender's profit or loss. Avoid: Sale value, realisation Taught in 001 · Lesson 4 In the code: 005 · Lesson 4
