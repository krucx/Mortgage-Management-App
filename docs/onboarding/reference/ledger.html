The Ledger engine, on one page

The rules backend/src/mortgage_backend/lending/ledger.py follows, for hand-tracing a Mortgage. Taught in chapter 004 (an open Mortgage) and chapter 005 (Outstanding and Closures, from lending/replay.py and lending/closing.py).

Stored vs derived

Stored (facts)Derived on every read (never stored)
Start date, amount lent, Terms; Term Changes; each non-Voided Payment's Allocation (date, amount, Payment id)Every LedgerStep: interest, Interest to Date, how each Payment divided, Capitalisations, Principal, unpaid interest, Months Unpaid, Projections

compute_ledger(start, amount_lent, terms, as_of, allocations, …, term_changes, hypothetical) replays from the start date every time. Allocations are sorted by (date, payment_id). An Allocation larger than everything owed on its date raises Overpayment. ADR 0001; trap #4.

One month's interest

RuleIn the code
Money is paise (integers). Rates are basis points per month: 200 = 2% a month.Allocation.amount, Terms.monthly_rate_bps
Charge = Principal × bps × days ÷ (10,000 × 30), rounded half-up to the whole rupee. A whole month is 30 days.monthly_interest, DAYS_IN_A_MONTH
The n-th Accrual Date is the start day, n months on, clamped to month-end; always counted from the start date.accrual_date, is_accrual_date
Interest to Date days = days since the last Accrual Date (or start) + 1: today always counts.interest_to_date_days
Part-Month Rule: pro-rata = min(days, 30) of 30; full month = the whole month from day 1; nothing = 0.interest_to_date_charge
A month, and its Interest to Date, are charged on the Principal at the start of that month._Run.month_start_principal; ADR 0006

The order inside a day

On each Accrual Date, in _Run.accrue_through:

  1. Payments dated before today (pay_through(on − 1 day)).
  2. The month just ended's interest, on its starting Principal, under the Terms in force when it began; unpaid += interest − paid_ahead.
  3. Decide whether to capitalise, on those same old Terms.
  4. The new month starts: month_start_principal = principal, paid_ahead = 0.
  5. Today's real Payments (pay_through(on)).
  6. A Projection's hypothetical Payment, if any (capped at what's owed; payment_id is None).
  7. Capitalisation of what's still unpaid, also added to month_start_principal.
  8. A term_changed step if the month starting today has new Terms.

After the last Accrual Date: the remaining Payments, then the Interest to Date step (charge_interest_to_date, shown only when non-zero). Trap #7.

What a Payment pays

OrderPaysNote
1Unpaid interestOne running total, oldest and newest alike
2The Interest to Date on its datePaid ahead (paid_ahead); counts against the month's interest when it's charged
3PrincipalLowers interest from the next month only. Nothing can pay later months' interest early.

months_unpaid reads only interest and payment steps: Payments cover months oldest first, a partly covered month counts as unpaid, capitalised interest still counts as unpaid, and the Interest to Date never counts.

Only interest still unpaid at the end of the Accrual Date is capitalised: a Payment that day avoids it. Interest paid ahead and the Interest to Date are never capitalised.

Term Changes and Projections

  • A TermChange(on, …) starts on an Accrual Date and changes only the fields it sets. terms_on(terms, changes, day) gives the Terms for a month starting on day. The first interest it changes is added one month after its date. Nothing earlier changes.
  • project(…, dates, …, hypothetical, paid_off_by) runs one _Run past today, taking a Snapshot on each date (Principal, unpaid interest, Outstanding with its Interest to Date). HypotheticalPayments(after, amount) pays amount on every Accrual Date after after.
  • GET /api/mortgages/{id}/projection (lending/projections.py): today and each month for a year, a "no more Payments" baseline and an optional monthly_payment what-if, the coming Term Changes, and paid_off_on within ten years.
  • A Projection replays; a Trend (the Dashboard's 3-month average) doesn't.

Outstanding

  • MortgageReplay.outstanding(on) = the last step of ledger(on): Principal + unpaid interest. The Interest to Date is inside the unpaid interest, so it's exactly what would Redeem that day. Nothing once closed (but a date before the Closure gives the open figure). ADR 0011.
  • replays(conn, rows) loads every Allocation (Voided ones left out at the query) and Term Change for all the rows in two queries. replay(conn, id) does one, and 404s.
  • ledger(as_of) is cached per as_of on the object, for one request only. Never keep a MortgageReplay across requests (trap #4).
  • Avoid "balance" and "Redemption Amount" (retired by ADR 0011).

Closing on a date

RuleIn the code
A quote is the Outstanding if closed on on, leaving out the Closure's own Final Payment. It splits out interest_to_date, its days, and last_accrual_on.outstanding_on_closure(on); GET /mortgages/{id}/outstanding-on-closure?on=
Refused (422), in this order: not Active ("already …"); before the start ("had not started"); any Payment strictly after on ("close it on or after then").not_closable(on)

On the closing day: accrue, that day's other Payments, then the Interest to Date (fixed from now on), then the Final Payment, then any write-off. Nothing accrues after closed_on.compute_ledger (closed_on, closure, final_payment)

Dormant and Reopen

  • dormant(on): Active and outstanding(on).total == 0. Derived, never stored; listed by GET /closures/dormant; no Reminders, off the Call List. Put right by Redeeming (no Final Payment) or correcting a Payment. ADR 0012.
  • reopen: 409 if Active. Clears status, closed_on, redemption_payment_id, recovered_amount, closure_note; then Voids the old Final Payment, if any, with the reason; History reopened.
  • A closed Mortgage refuses Payments, Voids and Term Changes (409 "… reopen it first"). Its Final Payment can't be Voided directly (409 "reopen the Mortgage instead, which voids it").

Simulations

  • simulate replays SimulationIn alone through compute_ledger, closed on Closed on (or the day the plan pays it off, if sooner), and reads it with outstanding_on_closure_of: the same reader as MortgageReplay.outstanding_on_closure. So a Simulation owes what a real Mortgage with those Payments would. No database.
  • Lump Sums become Allocations numbered by their place; the monthly payment is HypotheticalPayments. A Lump Sum after Closed on is ignored; one larger than what is owed is refused (422 naming it), unless the plan had already paid it off, when it is ignored.
  • Interest earned = each month's interest + the Interest to Date; Capitalised interest counted once. Limits: Closed on after the start and within 10 years of Accrual Dates; Lump Sums on or after the start.
  • The parity tests compare a Simulation with a real Mortgage, so they can't catch a bug in the shared reader; fixed-figure tests do.

Hand-trace checklist

  1. List the Accrual Dates from the start date (clamp to month-end).
  2. Sort the Payments by date, then Payment id.
  3. For each Accrual Date, follow the order above. Keep four numbers: Principal, unpaid interest, the month's starting Principal, paid ahead.
  4. End with the Interest to Date on the day you're asked about.
  5. Check against the Mortgage page (Show complete Ledger), or run compute_ledger from backend/ with uv run python.
