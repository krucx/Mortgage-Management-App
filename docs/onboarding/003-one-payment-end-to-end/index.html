Chapter 003

One Payment, end to end

The problem this chapter solves

You know the business, the map of the repo, and how to run it. But the app's layers are still separate boxes in your head: a React form here, a Python function there, a SQLite table somewhere else. A bug report won't tell you which box it's in. "Meera's Receipt shows the wrong amount" could be the form, the Preview, the save, the money rules, or the Receipt.

This chapter joins the boxes up. You make one real Payment for Meera, ₹6,600 by UPI across her bangles and her chain, on your own scratch book, with the browser's Network tab open, and follow it through every layer. Each lesson stays shallow and points to the chapter that goes deep.

What you'll be able to do

  • Name each layer a Payment crosses, and the four requests it makes.
  • Say where the draft is built, and how the Preview gets its figures without the frontend computing money.
  • Follow POST /api/payments from validation to committed rows, and say why a refusal saves nothing.
  • Explain why nothing owed is stored, and where a Payment's interest/Principal division comes from.
  • Follow the cache refresh, the Receipt and the WhatsApp send.

Before you start

Chapter 001 for Payments, Allocations, Receipts and Previews in plain words, and chapter 002 for the repo's layout and serving the sample book. You'll serve a fresh book in lesson 1 and keep it running through lesson 5. Use a browser with developer tools (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
