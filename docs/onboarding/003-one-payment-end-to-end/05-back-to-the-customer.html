Chapter 003 · Lesson 5

Back to the Customer

The Payment is saved. Now every screen that showed what Meera owes is out of date, and Meera is waiting for proof that the Lender got her ₹6,600. How does the app refresh itself, build the Receipt, and get it onto her phone?

By the end you can follow the refresh, the Receipt and the WhatsApp send.

The problem

Two problems, one on each side of the counter.

The Lender's screens. The frontend caches what it fetched: Meera's Mortgages, the Dashboard's totals, the Dormant count. After a Payment, all of those are stale. Working out exactly which ones a given Payment touched would be fiddly and easy to get wrong, and one missed cache means the Lender sees an old figure and trusts it.

The Customer. Meera needs a Receipt in words she recognises, on the app she uses: WhatsApp. But automating WhatsApp is fragile and against its terms.

The idea

After any change that can move a Ledger, mark every money-related cache stale at once: cheap, because the backend replays in milliseconds, and impossible to get wrong. Build the Receipt on the backend, from a replay, like every other figure. Then open WhatsApp with the message already typed and let the Lender press Send (ADR 0002).

The flow

The code

1. Mark everything stale

Lesson 2 showed the form's save going through changeLedger. That's this hook:

TanStack Query stores each answer under a query key, an array like ['mortgage', 2]. invalidateQueries marks every key that starts with the one given as stale. A stale query that's on screen is fetched again straight away; one that isn't waits until it's next shown. Previews are left for later on purpose: the one on screen is the Preview of the change just made, which the backend would now refuse.

2. The Receipt, built by the backend

Same pattern as lesson 4: stored amount, replayed interest, Principal and "still owed". The response also carries the Customer's phone numbers and text, the WhatsApp message, worded from the Receipt template in Settings.

3. Handing over to WhatsApp

SendOnWhatsApp (in components/WhatsApp.tsx, line 47) shows a number picker when the Customer has two numbers, and calls sender.send. Every message to a Customer goes through sender, so swapping click-to-send for an API later would touch one file.

Nuances

(a) Click-to-send is a decision, not a shortcut

The app only opens WhatsApp; the Lender presses Send. ADR 0002 explains why: driving WhatsApp automatically breaks whenever WhatsApp changes its screens, and breaks its terms, which could get the Lender's own number banned. Notices (chapter 009) use the same sender.

(b) Receipts name the jewellery, never the Mortgage Name

Predict: the Payment's page lists "2026-Meera-PairBangles". What does Meera's Receipt call it, and why?

"Pair of bangles", the Mortgage's description. A Mortgage Name and an id are the Lender's internal labels; anything a Customer reads uses the description and the Customer's name instead (line 96 of receipts.py, and the glossary's definition of Receipt). Keep that rule whenever you touch customer-facing text. Chapter 007, lesson 5 covers the Receipt's wording.

(c) One Payment, one Receipt, perhaps several rows

Meera's Payment is one payment row. But a Payment that Redeems a Mortgage keeps that Mortgage's Final Payment as a row of its own, pointing back with received_with, and load_receipt gathers all the parts into one Receipt (its lines 65–79). The API never shows received_with. Chapter 007, lesson 2 explains why.

Quiz

After the save, why isn't the Dashboard fetched again at once?

keys.dashboard is in REFRESHED_AFTER_LEDGER_CHANGE, so it is marked stale. TanStack Query only refetches stale queries that are being shown; the Dashboard's will be fetched when the Lender next opens it.

Where is the Receipt's "Still owed ₹81,733" worked out?

load_receipt replays each Mortgage and asks owed_after for the Payment. Nothing is stored, which is why a Receipt reopened months later still shows the figures as they stood just after the Payment.

What does the app do when the Lender clicks Send on WhatsApp?

clickToSend.send opens api.whatsapp.com/send/?phone=…&text=… in a new tab. The Lender presses Send there (ADR 0002). Nothing is queued or sent by the app.
