Chapter 007 · Lesson 3

Voids: how a Payment is corrected

People type the wrong number. This lesson shows the only way the app lets you correct a Payment, what that leaves behind, and the three Voids it refuses.

By the end you can correct a wrong Payment, and say which Voids are refused.

The problem

On 10 Oct Meera paid ₹6,000 in cash towards her bangles. The Lender typed ₹16,000. Within the hour they noticed. What should the fix look like?

The obvious fixes are "edit the amount to ₹6,000" or "delete the row and type it again". Both lose something that matters in a business that handles cash. After an edit, nothing shows that ₹16,000 was ever on record, and the bangles' Ledger silently changes under every figure the Lender has already told Meera. After a delete, a Receipt Meera might already have on her phone points at a Payment that no longer exists. If a figure is ever disputed, the Lender needs to see what was entered, when, and why it changed.

The idea

Never edit or delete a stored Payment. The only write allowed on one is the Void: it stamps the row with when and why, and from then on every replay leaves it out. A correction is two steps: Void the wrong Payment, then enter the right one as a new Payment.

The flow

Notice there's no arrow back. A Reopen does Void a Final Payment, but nothing ever un-Voids one.

The code

1. The request: a reason, always

min_length=1 alone would let " " through. The validator strips the spaces first, then refuses what's left if it's empty.

2. void_payment: refuse, write, check, History

  1. A Payment can be Voided once. A second Void is a conflict with its state, so 409.
  2. A Closure's Final Payment can't be Voided directly: that would leave a Redeemed or Waived Mortgage whose redemption_payment_id points at a Voided Payment. The Lender has to Reopen the Mortgage, which clears the Closure first and then Voids the Final Payment through this same function (chapter 006, lesson 2).
  3. Any other Payment to a closed Mortgage is refused too: its Closure was worked out with that Payment in place, so changing what was paid means Reopening first.
  1. Search the backend for update(payment_table): this is the only one. There's no delete(payment_table) anywhere, and nothing updates or deletes an allocation row.
  2. The reason is recorded as a change of void_reason from nothing to the reason, not as a note (chapter 006, lesson 3).

After a Void, a replay simply doesn't see the Payment: replays() loads only non-Voided Allocations (chapter 005, lesson 1). That's why load_payment skips the replay for a Voided Payment (lines 137–140, lesson 1) and shows interest_paid and principal_paid as null: it's in no Ledger, so it paid nothing.

Between the two steps, the bangles owe ₹6,000 more, because the Ledger no longer has that Payment. Step 2 is an ordinary back-dated Payment: it slots into the Ledger on 10 Oct, and every later figure is replayed with it. The Void's response comes from post_void (line 913), which Voids and then returns load_payment: the Payment, now with voided_at set and no split.

The Void has a Preview twin, POST /payments/{id}/void-preview (chapter 006 owns it). Try curl -s -X POST http://127.0.0.1:8765/api/payments/28/void-preview: the bangles go from ₹1,51,500 to ₹1,57,500, the 10 Oct payment step is removed, and nothing is saved.

(b) Voiding the ordinary part of a split Payment

Predict: take lesson 2's Payment (34: bangles ₹1,000; 35: the chain's ₹83,333 Final Payment, received_with 34). Void 34. Is it refused? What happens to 35 and to the chain?

It's allowed. 34 goes to the bangles, which are Active, and it isn't anyone's Final Payment. Only 34 is Voided. Payment 35 stays, still received_with 34, and the chain stays Redeemed with redemption_payment_id 35. The Receipt of 34 now answers 409; the Receipt of 35 covers just the chain: ₹83,333, nothing still owed.

A Void works on a part, never on a whole Payment. That's the same rule as the Reopen in lesson 2, from the other side.

(c) check_replays can't refuse a Void today

A Void only takes a Payment away, so what's owed afterwards can only go up. Chapter 006, lesson 2 shows that deleting line 364 fails no test. It stays so the Void follows the same four steps as every change.

Quiz

A ₹5,000 Payment should have been ₹500. What does the Lender do?

There is no edit or delete for a Payment, in the API or the screens. The only write to a stored Payment is the Void, which keeps it on record with its reason. The correct amount is a new Payment, back-dated to the real date.

Why can't you Void Kavita's Payment 26 directly?

void_payment refuses when redemption_payment_id is this Payment. Reopen the coin instead: the Reopen clears the Closure, then Voids Payment 26 through the same function. There's no age limit, and the method doesn't matter.

What does GET /api/payments/27 show for its Allocation's interest_paid?

load_payment skips the replay for a Voided Payment and sets both halves of the split to None. Nothing was ever stored, so there's no "what it paid before" to show.
