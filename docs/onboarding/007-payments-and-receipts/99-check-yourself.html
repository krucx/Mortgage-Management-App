Chapter 007 · Check yourself

Check yourself: Payments and Receipts

Questions from all six lessons, mixed up. Several are about one Payment: on 19 Oct, Arjun Patel hands over cash for ₹1,000 on his ruby ring and his gold chain's whole Outstanding, ₹46,830, listed ring first. Then one small break in the real code.

Quiz

1. Arjun's Payment is saved on a fresh book. How many payment rows does it make?

The ring's ₹1,000 is ordinary, so it's the first part (row 34). The chain's ₹46,830 is its whole Outstanding, so it Redeems the chain and is kept as a part of its own (row 35, received_with 34). There's no extra row for "the Payment": the first part is it. (Lesson 2; from scripts/run_throughs.py, section 99.)

2. What does the Preview of Arjun's Payment give as cash?

cash_sums adds each part to its first part's total (received_with or id), so the Final Payment is counted with the ₹1,000. ₹47,830 is above ₹20,000, so the Lender is asked "₹47,830 in cash" before saving. (Lesson 4.)

3. Where is the interest/Principal split of Meera's Payment 23 stored?

Neither table has a column for it, and History records only paid_on, amount, method and reference. load_payment reads it from the replay's step for that Payment, so a Payment typed in later and dated earlier changes it, correctly. (Lesson 1, ADR 0001.)

4. After saving Arjun's Payment, the Lender tries POST /payments/35/void. What answers?

Payment 35 is the chain's redemption_payment_id, so void_payment refuses: "This is the final Payment of the closing of 2026-Arjun-GoldChain: reopen the Mortgage instead, which voids it". Voiding 34, the ring's ordinary part, would be allowed, and would leave 35 alone. (Lesson 3.)

5. What does POST /api/payments return as the saved Payment's id?

record_payment always returns the first part, and the route answers with load_payment of it: amount ₹1,000, one Allocation. The Receipt gathers both parts. (Lesson 2, trap #2.)

6. Kavita's anklet owes ₹0 today. She brings ₹2,000 in cash for it. What does the Preview say?

₹2,000 isn't the anklet's whole Outstanding (₹0), so it's an ordinary Payment, and check_replays finds it larger than what was owed: "2026-Kavita-SilverGoldAnklet: the Payment on 19 Oct 2026 would be more than the ₹0 owed then". (Lessons 1 and 2; from scripts/run_throughs.py, section 99.)

7. On Arjun's Receipt, what is the chain's line titled, and what does it still owe?

A Receipt names each item by its description, never its Mortgage Name (trap #16), and adds "(Redeemed)" when the Allocation was a Redemption's Final Payment. The chain's line: ₹46,830 paid, ₹1,830 interest, ₹45,000 Principal, ₹0 still owed. The ring's line still owes ₹84,184. (Lesson 5.)

8. The Lender Forfeits Meera's chain, recovering ₹90,000 in cash from selling it. What does the Forfeit Preview give as cash?

cash_sums looks only at new payment rows and Principals lent in cash that are new or larger. A Forfeit records a recovered amount on the Mortgage, not a Payment. (Lesson 4.)

9. A Payment gives ₹2,000 cash and a ₹8,000 Discount to one Mortgage. What is reported as cash, and what happens if the Discount exceeds the remaining Outstanding?

Discount is non-cash and cash reports include only the Payment's cash amount. The Payment Preview reports each Allocation's room for a Discount and whether it is over the threshold; the screen asks before reducing an over-balance Discount to its room, and asks again above the threshold. (Chapters 007 lesson 1 and 012 lesson 3, ADR 0014.)

Recap

  • A Payment is one payment row and an allocation row per Mortgage, amounts in paise. PaymentIn refuses Allocations that don't add up or name a Mortgage twice; refuse_payment refuses a future date, another Customer's Mortgage, a closed one (409) and a date before the start; check_replays refuses a Payment larger than what was owed. The interest/Principal split is never stored: it's replayed (lesson 1).
  • A Payment that Redeems is stored in parts: the ordinary Allocations first, then each Final Payment as its own row with received_with = the first part. The id returned is the first part's. The API never shows received_with; only record_payment sets it (lesson 2, trap #2).
  • A Payment is never edited or deleted. A correction is a Void with a reason plus a new Payment. A Void is refused if already Voided, if it's a Closure's Final Payment (Reopen instead), or if the Mortgage is closed. A Voided Payment stays listed, has no Receipt and no split (lesson 3).
  • The Cash Limit only warns. Each Preview lists what the change writes in cash: each new Payment as one sum with its Final Payments, and each Principal lent in cash that's new, newly in cash, or larger. The page asks at or above the limit; saving checks nothing. No stored value means ₹20,000; an empty one means never warn (lesson 4).
  • A Receipt gathers every non-Voided part, gives one line per Mortgage by its description, and shows what each owed just after the Payment, replayed each time, so a resent Receipt matches unless an earlier fact changed. A Waiver's line owes nothing; a Redemption is marked "(Redeemed)"; the Lender presses Send (lesson 5, trap #16, ADR 0002).

Keep the routes sheet open when you work on Payments: it lists each Payment, Void, Receipt and Cash Limit route with its refusals. The ledger sheet now says what a Payment's Allocations pay and how a Payment's parts group, and the traps sheet marks #2 and #16 as this chapter's.
