Prologue

The Lender's morning

The Lender opens the app on their laptop. The first page is the whole lending book on one screen: what it is owed, what came in, what the gold behind it is worth, and what needs doing today. None of these figures was typed in. Each is replayed from the Mortgages' Terms and Payments every time the page opens.

Chapter 1

House rules

Before lending anything, the Lender sets their rules once in Settings: the usual Terms, today's gold prices, when a Customer needs a phone call, the wording of WhatsApp messages, and Backups. Every save shows what will change and asks first.

Chapter 2

Meera walks in

Meera was introduced by Ravi Verma, who isn't a Customer himself: a Standalone Referrer, known only by name and phone. The Lender adds her, and the app makes sure she is never entered twice. A person is known by their mobile number, their Primary Number.

Chapter 3

Weighing the gold

In November 2025 Meera pledged a gold chain with a pendant: 21.5 g gross, 20 g of 22K gold, against ₹80,000. One packet of jewellery is one Mortgage. Here the Lender is entering her next one. The app suggests a Mortgage Name and shows what the loan will grow to before anything is saved.

How the Interest Mode changes what is owed

Try it

With no Payments. Simple: interest never earns interest. Compounded Yearly: interest is simple each month, then added to the Principal every twelve months. Compounded Monthly: added every month. The app's Ledger does the same, step by step, to the paisa.

Accrual Dates follow the start dateStarted on the 31st? Interest is added on the 31st, or on the month's last day when it is shorter.
Collateral Value from the weightNet weight at the Gold Rate for its purity: Meera's 20 g of 22K at ₹13,536 a gram is ₹2,70,720.
Names never clashA second chain the same year becomes …-GoldChain-2 on its own.
Chapter 4

The life of a pledge

Everything about Meera's chain is on one page. Each month on the 10th, 2% of ₹80,000 (₹1,600) is added. She has paid most months, but she missed April, and October's ₹1,600 was added on the 10th, so ₹2,800 of interest is unpaid today.

Interest on start-of-month PrincipalEach month is charged on the Principal at the previous Accrual Date, so paying Principal a day before an Accrual Date doesn't avoid a month's interest.
Nothing owed is storedOutstanding, the Ledger and Redemption Amounts are replayed from the facts, so a correction anywhere shows everywhere at once.
Chapter 5

Second thoughts, with a Preview

Suppose the chain's Principal was really ₹90,000. Any change that affects money shows its effect before it is saved: what is owed today, the Redemption Amount in 3, 6 and 12 months, and exactly which Ledger steps would change. The Preview is the real change, made by the backend with every check a save runs, then undone, so it can never disagree with saving.

Chapter 7

Meanwhile, Arjun

Arjun pledged a ruby ring in June 2025 at 2.5% a month, Compounded Monthly. He paid once, in August 2025. Fifteen months of compounding later he owes ₹84,831 against gold worth ₹81,216. The app never sends anything by itself: each morning it prepares a Call List and Notices, and the Lender decides.

His ring is 6 g of 22K gold. Loan-to-Value is what is owed ÷ the Collateral Value; Months Unpaid counts monthly interest not yet covered, oldest first. Either reason is enough (3 Months Unpaid or more, or above the Loan-to-Value limit), and so is accruing today.

Notices go out ahead of each Accrual Date: one per Customer, covering every Mortgage of theirs accruing that day. Because the Lender chose to approve them first, Sunita's waits for a look.

Chapter 8

How pledges end

Every pledge ends one of three ways. It is paid in full and the jewellery goes home (Redeemed). Or the Customer pays nearly all of it, the Lender lets off the small balance left, and the jewellery goes home all the same (Waived off). Or the Customer defaults and the Lender keeps or sells it (Forfeited). This month has one of each, and one more waiting.

Waive off a small balanceOn a Mortgage, Waive off takes what the Customer pays now as the final Payment (or nothing) and lets off the rest. Their Receipt shows nothing still owed, and the Dashboard counts what was let off for the month.
Reopen undoes completelyWith a reason, the Mortgage is Active again as if never closed, and a Redemption's or Waiver's final Payment is Voided.
No closing before a PaymentA Closure can't be dated before a Payment already recorded on that Mortgage.
Epilogue

Nothing is lost

Every change is kept in the History, with when it happened and what it was before. Nobody can edit or remove it. The whole book is backed up to a folder that leaves the laptop, weekly and before every update.

Getting the app onto the laptop is a double-click too. On Windows, Setup fetches the latest release and puts a Mortgage Management shortcut on the Desktop. With Docker Desktop it runs the published app in Docker; without, it installs what the app needs and runs it on the PC itself. Running Setup again brings the newest release, and the book and its Backups stay as they are. The install guide has the download and every step.

The year, in six lines

  1. The whole book on one screen, worked out fresh every morning.
  2. The Lender's rules set once, applied to every new pledge.
  3. Each person entered once, known by their number and who introduced them.
  4. Every effect seen before it is saved, and every save confirmed.
  5. Payments split fairly, Receipts on WhatsApp, follow-up prepared daily for the Lender to send.
  6. Every pledge closed with an exact amount, and every change kept.
