Mortgage Book Walkthrough

One Lender, one laptop, a book of gold-jewellery pledges. This guide follows a pledge through its whole life in the app: the morning view of the book, setting the rules, meeting a Customer, lending against their jewellery, taking Payments, following up, and closing the Mortgage. Each stop says where to click, what to say, and the details worth pointing out.

Before you start: this is the real book

  • Everything you open, search, sort or preview is safe. A Preview works out the change and throws it away.
  • Every save asks first. To show a save without making it, stop at the confirmation and press Keep editing or Cancel.
  • This guide points to Mortgages and Payments by their page address (for example /mortgages/10) rather than naming Customers, so it can be shared.

Settings: the Lender's rules

Open /settings

"Before any lending, the Lender sets their house rules once: the usual Terms, today's gold prices, when someone needs a phone call, the wording of WhatsApp messages, and Backups."

Show, top to bottom
  1. Default Terms: copied into every new Mortgage. Changing them never touches existing Mortgages.
  2. Gold Rates: price per gram for each purity, with the date last updated. Saving revalues every Mortgage that uses them.
  3. Call List: how many Months Unpaid, or how high a Loan-to-Value, puts a Mortgage on the day's Call List.
  4. WhatsApp messages: the Receipt and Notice templates with their placeholders, a live preview, how many days ahead Notices are queued, and whether each needs approving first.
  5. Backups: when the last one was taken, Back up now, and how many to keep.

Customers and Referrers

Open /customers, then a Customer

"A Customer is a person, known by their mobile number. The app makes sure one person is never entered twice, and remembers who introduced them."

Show
  1. The list: filter by name or phone as you type, tick Only Customers with an Active Mortgage, sort any column. Matching Referrers appear alongside.
  2. New Customer: type a contact number 1 that already belongs to someone. The form warns who owns it before you save.
  3. The Referrer choice: None, an Existing person (search), or a New Referrer known only by name and phone.
  4. Open a Customer: photo, numbers, who referred them, whom they referred, their Active Mortgages with closed ones tucked below, and their History.
  5. Stop before saving: press Keep editing.

Lending: a New Mortgage

Open /mortgages/new (or New Mortgage on a Customer)

"Lending is one Mortgage per packet of jewellery. The Lender picks the Customer, sees what they already owe, weighs the gold, and the app suggests a name and shows what this loan will grow to before anything is saved."

Show
  1. Choose a Customer. Their Active Mortgages appear first, as a reminder of what they already owe.
  2. Enter the start date (it may be back-dated), Principal lent, a description, gross and net weight, and purity.
  3. Watch the Mortgage Name fill itself: year, the Customer's first name, and the jewellery (for example 2026-Name-GoldNecklace). Type your own and it stops suggesting.
  4. The Terms arrive from Settings and can be changed for this one Mortgage. Add photos from the camera or files.
  5. Press Create Mortgage: the confirmation shows what it would owe today and over a year with no Payments. Press Keep editing.

One Mortgage, explained

Open /mortgages/10 (Compounded Monthly), then /mortgages/3 (a coming Term Change)

"Everything about one pledge is on one page: what it owes now, the Terms and any coming changes, what the gold is worth, every call made, the photos, and the full step-by-step Ledger showing how the number was reached."

Show, top to bottom
  1. The three tiles: Outstanding, Principal, Unpaid interest. Mortgage 10 lent ₹1,20,000 in April 2025 and owes ₹1,98,342 today.
  2. Terms: on Mortgage 3, a Term Change is coming from 1 Mar 2027 (10% a month) and can still be removed.
  3. Collateral Value and Loan-to-Value, with Set by hand…. The confirmation says whether that puts it on or off the Call List.
  4. Ledger: the latest 8 steps; Show complete Ledger opens all of them. Each line spells out its calculation ("Interest: 3% of ₹…").
  5. Projection: type a monthly amount in "What if they pay every month" to see when it would be paid off.
  6. History: every change, with old and new values.

Changes, with a Preview first

From a Mortgage: Edit (/mortgages/10/edit) and Change Terms… (/mortgages/10/terms)

"Any change that affects money shows its effect before it is saved: what is owed today, the Redemption Amount in 3, 6 and 12 months, and exactly which Ledger lines would change. If the change isn't allowed, the Preview says why and Save stays off."

Show
  1. On Edit, change the Principal lent. What changes appears: Owed today before → after, interest over 12 months, a before/after chart, and the changed Ledger steps.
  2. Change it back: the Preview disappears and Save turns off. Only real changes can be saved.
  3. On Change Terms, pick an Accrual Date and a new rate. The Preview shows the effect from that date only.
  4. Press Save to show the confirmation, then Keep editing.

Payments, Receipts and Voids

Open /payments/new, then /payments and a Payment (/payments/4 is split three ways)

"A Customer often hands over one sum for several pledges. The Lender enters it once, the app suggests how to split it, shows what each Mortgage will owe afterwards, and sends the Customer a Receipt on WhatsApp."

Show
  1. Choose the Customer. Their Active Mortgages are listed with what each owes on the Payment date, so a back-dated Payment sees back-dated figures.
  2. Enter the amount, or click The full Outstanding on the chosen Mortgages. Split it Equally, By amount or By percentage; ticked Mortgages stay together at the top.
  3. Type more than is owed: the Preview gives the reason ("would be more than the ₹… owed then") and Save stays off.
  4. Open an existing Payment: each Allocation split into interest and Principal, the Receipt as it appears in WhatsApp, and Print or save as PDF.
  5. Show Void…: it needs a reason and previews the effect. Cancel.
  6. The Payments list: filter by month or year, hide Voided ones; a row opens its Payment.

Follow-up: Notices and the Call List

Open /notices, then /call-list

"The app never sends anything by itself. It prepares reminders and a calling list each day; the Lender reviews them and presses Send in WhatsApp."

Show
  1. Notices: one per Customer for each coming Accrual Date, covering all their Mortgages accruing that day. Tabs: To approve, To send, Sent, Skipped, All.
  2. Open a Notice to read the message, then Send on WhatsApp, mark it sent, or Skip. On the To send tab, each row has a compact green WhatsApp Send beside Skip. (Show, don't send.)
  3. Call List: one table, a row per Mortgage, grouped by Customer. The Customer appears once beside their rows, with how many Mortgages are listed, their total, and a Call button showing each number next to a WhatsApp chat button.
  4. Point to the Why markers: amber for Months Unpaid ("15 mo"), red for Loan-to-Value too high ("104.5%"), teal for accruing today ("Today"), with the legend above the table. Hover one for the full wording.
  5. Click a Customer's 2 Mortgages: the Mortgages page opens with only theirs, and Show every Customer goes back.
  6. Mark called… takes a note of what was said; the row then shows ✓ Called with the note. The Mortgage keeps every Call Note.

Closing a Mortgage: Redeem, Waive off or Forfeit

Open /closures (Redeem / Forfeit), and Close this Mortgage on /mortgages/4

"Every pledge ends one of three ways: the Customer pays in full and takes the jewellery home; they pay nearly all of it and the Lender, at their discretion, lets off the small balance left and hands the jewellery back; or they default and the Lender keeps or sells it. The app works out the exact Redemption Amount for any date, records what was let off on a Waiver, and the profit or loss on a Forfeiture."

Show
  1. Point to the dot on Redeem / Forfeit in the menu: a Mortgage is paid off and waiting for its jewellery to go back.
  2. On the page, Ready to Redeem lists it (Mortgage 4 owes ₹0). Below, under Forfeit or Waive off, Active Mortgages still owing can be ticked and closed together: choose Forfeit or Waive off for the chosen.
  3. On a Mortgage, press Redeem…: pick a date to see the Redemption Amount (Principal, unpaid interest, part-month charge). A future date gives a quote only.
  4. Switch to Waive off…: enter what the Customer pays now (₹0 is allowed) and the amount let off appears, with the button reading Waive off ₹…. Paying the full amount is refused: Redeem instead. Cancel.
  5. Switch to Forfeit…: enter the Recovered Amount and the profit or loss appears. Cancel.
  6. On a closed Mortgage, show Reopen…: it needs a reason, and its Preview shows interest charged for the whole time.

Safekeeping: History and Backups

The History on any Mortgage or Customer, and /settings → Backups

"Nothing is ever silently changed or lost. Every change is in the History with what it was before, and the whole book is backed up to a folder that leaves this laptop."

Show
  1. On a Mortgage, the History: each edit with old → new values, Term Changes added and removed, photos, Voids and Reopens with their reasons.
  2. On a Customer, the History also includes their Mortgages' changes.
  3. In Settings, the last Backup's time and file, and the warning if Backups are not password-protected.
