Mortgage Management

A lending book for a jeweller who lends against gold. One Lender keeps every Customer, every pledged packet of jewellery, every Payment and every follow-up in one app on their own laptop. The app works out what each Mortgage owes on any date, prepares the day's Notices and phone calls, and keeps every change.

Version: 1.4.0 Runs on: the Lender's laptop, Windows or anywhere with Docker Opens at: http://localhost:8080 Money: ₹, Indian digit grouping

What it is

A Customer pledges a packet of jewellery and borrows against it. That loan is a Mortgage. Interest is added each month on its Accrual Date. Payments come in, sometimes one sum for several Mortgages. Each Mortgage ends when the jewellery goes home or is kept.

CustomerKnown by their Primary Number, maybe introduced by a Referrer
MortgageOne packet of jewellery, weighed and valued, on its Terms
Accrual DatesInterest added monthly; capitalised by the Interest Mode
Follow-upNotices before each Accrual Date; the Call List each day
PaymentsOne sum split across Mortgages, interest first
ClosureRedeemed or Waived off and handed back, or Forfeited

It is

  • For one Lender, on their own laptop. Nobody else can reach it, and there is no sign-in.
  • Exact: what is owed is worked out from the Terms and Payments every time it is shown, never typed in.
  • Careful: every change that affects money shows its effect first, and every save is confirmed.
  • Kept: every change is in the History, and the whole book is backed up to a folder that leaves the laptop.

It isn't

  • A website or cloud service. The data is a folder on the laptop.
  • A messenger that sends by itself. It prepares WhatsApp messages; the Lender presses Send.
  • A place where records disappear. Payments are Voided and Closures Reopened, with a reason, never deleted.
  • Multi-user or multi-branch. There is one Lender and one book.

Workflows

The Lender's everyday jobs, step by step. Open one to see it.

Lend against new jewelleryA new or returning Customer pledges a packet
  1. New to the book? Customers → New Customer. Enter their name and contact number 1, and their Referrer if someone introduced them. The form warns if the number already belongs to someone.
  2. On the Customer, press New Mortgage. Their Active Mortgages show first, as a reminder of what they already owe.
  3. Enter the start date (back-dating is fine), the Principal lent, a description of the jewellery, gross and net weight, and purity. Add photos.
  4. Check the suggested Mortgage Name and the Terms copied from Settings; change either for this Mortgage if needed.
  5. Press Create Mortgage. The confirmation shows what it will owe today and a year from now with no Payments. Confirm.

Two packets mean two Mortgages, even for the same Customer on the same day: each has its own Terms, Payments and Closure.

Take a PaymentOne sum, perhaps for several Mortgages, and a Receipt
  1. Payments → New Payment, and choose the Customer. Set the date the money came in; the figures shown are as of that date.
  2. Enter the amount and the method (Cash, UPI or bank transfer), or press The full Outstanding on the chosen Mortgages.
  3. Tick the Mortgages it is for and split it Equally, By amount or By percentage. The Preview shows what each will owe afterwards.
  4. Record it, then open the Receipt: Send on WhatsApp or Print or save as PDF.
  5. If it cleared a Mortgage, the app offers Redeem now: accept to close it and hand the jewellery back.

Entered the wrong amount? Open the Payment, press Void… with a reason, and record it again.

Each morning: follow upNotices to send and calls to make
  1. Open the app. The Dashboard's Follow-up tile shows today's Call List and the Notices waiting.
  2. Notices: on To approve (if approve-first is on), read each and approve or skip it. On To send, press the green WhatsApp Send; WhatsApp opens with the message ready.
  3. Call List: read why each Mortgage is listed, then Call or open a WhatsApp chat.
  4. After each call, Mark called… and note what was said.

The first time the app is opened each day, its daily run queues that day's Notices and takes the weekly Backup when one is due. Days the app wasn't opened are made up then.

Change a Mortgage's rateA Term Change from an Accrual Date onwards
  1. On the Mortgage, press Change Terms….
  2. Pick the Accrual Date it starts on, and the new rate, Interest Mode or Part-Month Rule.
  3. Read the Preview: the effect from that date only. Save and confirm.

A Term Change still to come can be removed. Once it has taken effect it stays; add another one to change again. To change the rate for future Mortgages, change the Default Terms in Settings instead.

Gold prices movedRevalue the book, and see who is now at risk
  1. Settings → Gold Rates: enter today's Gold Rate per gram for each purity and save. Every Mortgage valued by weight is revalued at once.
  2. The Dashboard's Loan-to-Value and the Call List now use the new values.
  3. For a packet worth more or less than its weight says, use Set by hand… on its Collateral Value. The confirmation says whether that puts it on or off the Call List.
Close a MortgageRedeem, Waive off or Forfeit
  1. Redeem / Forfeit in the menu shows a dot when a Mortgage is paid off and waiting for its jewellery to go back.
  2. Redeem: on the Mortgage, Redeem…, pick the date and record the Redemption Amount (Principal, unpaid interest and any part-month charge) as the final Payment.
  3. Waive off: when the Customer pays nearly all and the Lender lets the rest go, enter what they paid now (₹0 is allowed). The amount let off is shown and recorded.
  4. Forfeit: when the Customer has defaulted, enter the Recovered Amount the jewellery fetched or is worth. The profit or loss is shown.
  5. To close several at once, tick them under Forfeit or Waive off on the Redeem / Forfeit page.

Closed by mistake? Reopen… with a reason makes it Active as if it was never closed, and Voids the final Payment.

Fix a mistakeWithout losing the record of what happened
  1. Wrong start date or Principal: Edit the Mortgage. The Preview shows every Ledger line that changes. (Reopen a closed Mortgage first.)
  2. Wrong Payment: Void… it with a reason, then record the right one.
  3. Wrong Closure: Reopen… with a reason, then close it again correctly.
  4. Wrong Customer details: edit the Customer. A Customer with no Mortgages can be deleted.

Every one of these is in the History, with the old and new values and the reason.

Install, update, back upLooking after the app itself
  1. Install on Windows: download Setup.bat, double-click it, answer its questions, and use the Desktop shortcut. Elsewhere, Docker Compose.
  2. Update: run Setup.bat again. It backs up first, keeps the data, and brings the database up to date.
  3. Back up: Backups run weekly by themselves; press Back up now in Settings before anything risky. Point the Backup folder at Google Drive, iCloud or OneDrive so they leave the laptop.
  4. Remove: Uninstall.bat moves the latest Backup into Documents before deleting anything.

Every step, for your own computer, is in the install guide.

Rules and nuances

The details that decide the figures, and the guarantees behind them. The highlighted ones are the rules most worth knowing.

Interest

Charged on start-of-month PrincipalEach month's interest is on the Principal as it stood at the previous Accrual Date, so paying Principal the day before an Accrual Date doesn't skip a month.ADR-0006
Accrual Dates follow the start dateA Mortgage started on the 31st accrues on the 31st, or on the month's last day when it is shorter.
Three Interest ModesSimple: interest never earns interest. Compounded Yearly: simple each month, unpaid interest moved into Principal every year. Compounded Monthly: moved in every month.
The Part-Month RuleWhat is charged for the days since the last Accrual Date when a Mortgage is redeemed: pro-rata, a full month, or nothing.
Term Changes never rewrite the pastThey start on an Accrual Date and apply from then on. Changing the Default Terms never touches existing Mortgages.
Capitalised interest still counts as unpaidFor Months Unpaid, interest moved into Principal is still interest the Customer hasn't paid. Payments clear the oldest first.

Payments and Closures

Interest first, then PrincipalWithin each Mortgage, an Allocation pays unpaid interest before it reduces Principal.
No paying more than is owedA Payment larger than a Mortgage owes on its date is refused, with the reason.
The final Payment includes the part-month chargeRedeeming records the Redemption Amount as a Payment, so the Receipt and the Ledger agree.
A Waiver is the Lender's choiceWhat the Customer pays is the final Payment; the rest is let off and counted on the Dashboard. Paying the full amount is a Redemption, not a Waiver.
No closing before a later PaymentA Closure can't be dated before a Payment already recorded on that Mortgage.
Closed means lockedA closed Mortgage takes no Payments, accrues nothing, and its start date and Principal can't be edited until it is Reopened.

The figures you can trust

Nothing owed is storedOutstanding, the Ledger and Redemption Amounts are replayed from the Terms and Payments every time, so a correction anywhere shows everywhere at once.ADR-0001
A Preview is the real change, undoneThe change is made with every check a save would run, read, then rolled back. A Preview can't disagree with the save.
Exact versus estimateProjections replay the Mortgages and are exact; Trends carry the last three months forward and are labelled as estimates.
History is never editedEvery change to a Customer, Mortgage or Payment is kept, with old and new values, and can't be changed or removed.
Voided, never deletedA Voided Payment stays on record with its reason but counts in no Ledger.

People

The Primary Number identifies a personContact number 1 is unique: no two people share it. Number 2 may be shared, such as a family phone; the app warns but allows it.ADR-0005
Referrers grow into CustomersA Standalone Referrer who later borrows becomes a Customer, keeping everyone they referred. Nobody can refer themselves.
Lending history is never lostA Customer can be deleted only while they have no Mortgages.

Messages

The app never sends by itselfWhatsApp opens with the message ready and the Lender presses Send. No WhatsApp account setup or API is needed.ADR-0002
Customers see their jewellery, not codesReceipts and Notices name each Mortgage by its description ("Gold chain"), never its Mortgage Name.
Templates can't breakA template with a placeholder the app can't fill is refused on save, so a Customer never receives {typo}.
Notices always match todayUntil it is sent, a Notice's amounts and wording are worked out each time it is read, so a later Payment, edit or Closure shows in it. Turning on approve-first holds those already queued.

Running it

Only this laptop can reach itThe app listens on the laptop alone, and the data is a folder on its disk.ADR-0004
Backups leave the laptopA zip of the database and photos, into a folder synced by Google Drive, iCloud or OneDrive.ADR-0003
Docker or notWith Docker Desktop, Backups are password-protected zips. Without it, Setup installs Node.js and uv, the shortcut's window must stay open, and Backups are plain zips.
Every update backs up firstStarting a new version takes a Backup, then brings the database up to date. Updating keeps all data.

Words the app uses

Each word means one thing, everywhere in the app and these pages. The full list is the glossary.

Lender
The one person who runs the app and lends against pledged jewellery.
Customer
A person who has borrowed by pledging jewellery.
Referrer
Someone who introduced a Customer: another Customer, or a person known only as a Referrer.
Mortgage
One loan against one pledged packet of jewellery.
Terms
A Mortgage's rate, Interest Mode and Part-Month Rule.
Principal
What interest is charged on; grows when unpaid interest is capitalised, shrinks when Payments exceed interest.
Accrual Date
The monthly day a month's interest is added.
Ledger
The step-by-step history of a Mortgage's interest, capitalisations and Payments.
Outstanding
What a Mortgage owes on a date: Principal plus unpaid interest.
Redemption Amount
What must be paid on a date to redeem: the Outstanding plus any part-month charge.
Collateral Value
What the jewellery is worth now: its net weight at the Gold Rate, unless set by hand.
Loan-to-Value
The Outstanding as a share of the Collateral Value.
Allocation
The part of a Payment applied to one Mortgage.
Receipt
The Customer's acknowledgement of a Payment.
Notice
A message to a Customer ahead of an Accrual Date, covering every Mortgage accruing that day.
Months Unpaid
How many of the latest monthly interest charges are still unpaid.
Void
Cancelling a Payment with a reason, keeping it on record.
Redeemed · Waived · Forfeited
The three ways a Mortgage closes: paid in full, the rest let off, or the jewellery kept.
